## 🚨 GUARDRAILS (Don't Break These)

### Do NOT:

1. **Mention prices** — CRO-RJ forbids it ❌
   - Every change: check BRAND.md Seção 7

2. **Use words/phrases on the banned list** ❌
   - "sorriso perfeito", "premium", "excelência", "humanizado"
   - Every copy: search for these + remove

3. **Claim Dra. Carla does things she doesn't** ❌
   - Canal = endodontist (she coordinates, doesn't do)
   - Gengiva = periodontist (she coordinates, doesn't do)
   - Only: Implantes, Prótese, Lentes, Clareamento, Restaurações, Clínica Geral

4. **Break the tracking pipeline** ❌
   - GCLID must still be captured
   - dataLayer.push must still fire
   - Google Ads conversion tag must still fire
   - Test every change in GA4 + Google Ads

5. **Duplicate content across URLs** ❌
   - Service pages (organic) vs. LPs (paid) — both OK (different audience)
   - But no duplicate other than that
   - always use canonical tags

6. **Forget mobile** ❌
   - Mobile is majority of traffic
   - Test every change on iPhone SE, Samsung S21

## 💭 FAQs DURING EXECUTION

**Q: What if a pilar finding is concerning (e.g., design is worse than expected)?**
A: Report immediately to Patrick/Dra. Carla, don't wait for weekly. Document in STATUS with 🔴 flag.

**Q: What if we discover a quick win that wasn't planned?**
A: Bring to brainstorm meeting or Friday sync. If truly urgent, can prioritize immediately (with Dra. Carla approval).

**Q: What if Dra. Carla disagrees with a finding?**
A: It's her brand. Document her feedback, adjust recommendation. Move forward together.

**Q: What if a sprint takes longer than 2 weeks?**
A: Extend it, but communicate early. Update roadmap. Maybe deprioritize less important items.

**Q: What about other work (not this review)?**
A: This review is PRIMARY for Patrick (20-25h/week) and IA (25-30h/week). Other work paused or deferred.
